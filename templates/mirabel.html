In a world full of pride, be the humility.

In a world full of judgment, be the understanding.

In a world full of mistakes, be the forgiveness.

Let us not forget that we have the power to shape the little space around us. When darkness surrounds you, you have the power to be the light that others need. Be the good that brings on some hope.

Your actions, your choices, and your heart can influence others if we all try to be the positive one. In a world full of negatives, we can all create a ripple effect of kindness, understanding, and peace.

Keep shining, keep loving, and keep spreading good. Thanks.
