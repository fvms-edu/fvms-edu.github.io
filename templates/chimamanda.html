Kid's Health Corner

As a future nurse, here are some fun and easy health tips for all my schoolmates to stay strong and healthy:

  • The Scrub-A-Dub Rule: Always wash your hands with soap and clean water for 20 seconds before eating and after playing!
  • The Magic Water Filter: Drinking plenty of water every day helps your body run fast and gives you lots of energy.
  • Human Body Fun Fact: Did you know your heart is a powerful muscle that pumps blood around your body all day and night? It never takes a break!
